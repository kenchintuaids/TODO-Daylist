FONTS
-----
Daybook is designed for two fonts that I can't include in the project (one is commercial, the
other is free for personal use only). Add your own copies here and they are picked up automatically:

  Akira Expanded (headings, buttons, tabs)
      AkiraExpanded.woff2     (or AkiraExpanded.otf / AkiraExpanded.ttf)

  ITC Benguiat Std Medium Condensed (body text)
      BenguiatMdCn.woff2      (or BenguiatMdCn.otf / BenguiatMdCn.ttf)

Keep these exact file names. woff2 is best for the web; if you only have .otf/.ttf, that works too
(or convert at https://transfonter.org).

Licensing: Akira Expanded is free for personal use; ITC Benguiat Std is a commercial font and needs a
web license before you put it on a public site. If you only use the app on your own computer and the
fonts are installed there, they are used automatically even without these files.

Until the files are here, close stand-ins are used: Syncopate (wide caps) and Roboto Serif (condensed).
